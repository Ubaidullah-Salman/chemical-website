Uses of Carbon Black

Tyre & Rubber Reinforcement

Significantly improves tensile strength, abrasion resistance, and road endurance in tyres, conveyor belts, and automotive rubber mountings.

Plastics Masterbatch & Pipes

Provides deep black colour, high jetness, and critical protection against UV weathering in PE pipes, geomembranes, and agricultural mulch film.

Printing Inks & Coatings

High tinting strength black pigment used in newspaper, offset, flexographic inks, and industrial protective coatings.

Footwear & Industrial Goods

Imparts wear resistance, flex endurance, and structural firmness to shoe soles, hoses, gaskets, and floor mats.
