Uses of Acetic Acid

Textile Processing & Dyeing

Key pH buffer and neutralizer in dye baths for polyester, nylon, and wool fibers, ensuring bright, uniform colour uptake.

Leather Tanning & Pickling

Regulates acidity in pelt deliming, pickling, and tanning baths to prepare high-quality leather hides.

Chemical Synthesis

Crucial raw material for synthesizing vinyl acetate monomer (VAM), cellulose acetate, acetic anhydride, and ester solvents.

Industrial Descaling & Solvents

Used in specialty cleaning, descaling industrial pipelines, and formulating coatings and adhesives.

What is Glacial Acetic Acid?

Glacial Acetic Acid is undiluted acetic acid containing less than 0.15% water (purity >= 99.85%). It is called glacial because it freezes into ice-like crystals below 16.6°C (62°F).
