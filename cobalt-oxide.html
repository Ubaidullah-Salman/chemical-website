About this chemical

Potteries, tile makers and glass workshops use a little of this powder for blue colour. We also supply copper oxide if you need both.

Questions

What colour does cobalt oxide give?

It gives a deep blue in glaze and glass, even when you use a small amount.

What grades of cobalt oxide do you supply?

We supply Co₃O₄ (tricobalt tetroxide) and CoO (cobaltous oxide) grades. Tell us your application — ceramics, glass colouring, catalysts or pigment — and we will confirm the right grade.
