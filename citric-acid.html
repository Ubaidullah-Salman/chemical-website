Uses of Citric Acid

Food & Beverage Processing

Main acidulant and flavour enhancer for carbonated drinks, juices, jams, confectionery, and natural food preservation.

Pharmaceutical Formulations

Effervescent agent in dispersible tablets, acidity regulator in medicinal syrups, and anticoagulant buffer solutions.

Descaling & Industrial Cleaning

Powerful, biodegradable chelating agent that removes limescale from boilers, cooling systems, and plumbing fixtures.

Cosmetics & Personal Care

Adjusts pH balance in shampoos, lotions, facial cleansers, bath fizzers, and soaps.

What is the difference between Anhydrous and Monohydrate citric acid?

Anhydrous has zero water molecules in its crystal structure (dry powder), preferred in moisture-sensitive formulations like tablets. Monohydrate contains one water molecule per citric acid molecule and is widely used in syrups, beverages, and liquid cleaners.

Is your citric acid certified food grade?

Yes, we supply BP/USP/Food Grade certified citric acid that complies with international standards for food processing and pharmaceuticals.
