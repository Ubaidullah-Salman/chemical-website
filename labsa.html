Uses of LABSA

Laundry Washing Powders

Primary active cleaning agent delivering rich lather, outstanding stain penetration, and grease removal in detergent powders.

Dishwash & Liquid Cleaners

Neutralized with caustic soda or TEA to form sodium alkylbenzene sulfonate for clear dishwashing liquids and floor cleaners.

Industrial Degreasers & Scouring

Formulated into heavy-duty degreasers for machinery, vehicle wash bays, and textile scouring operations.

Agricultural Pesticide Emulsifiers

Serves as an effective anionic wetting agent and emulsifier in agricultural crop spray formulations.

What is the active matter content of your LABSA?

Our LABSA contains a minimum of 96% active anionic matter, with very low free sulfuric acid (< 1.5%) and low free oil (< 1.5%), ensuring light colour and easy neutralization.

What containers do you deliver in?

We deliver in 210 kg / 220 kg sealed heavy-gauge HDPE drums, as well as 1000 kg IBC containers for high-volume detergent plants.

Can you supply samples for lab titration?

Yes, sample containers are available for quality testing and formulation trials prior to bulk ordering.

Do you deliver across Pakistan?

Yes, we dispatch directly to detergent manufacturing units across Lahore, Karachi, Gujranwala, Multan, and Rawalpindi.
