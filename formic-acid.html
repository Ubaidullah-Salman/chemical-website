About this chemical

Formic acid (methanoic acid) is the simplest carboxylic acid. We supply high-grade 85% concentration to tanneries in Sialkot and Kasur, textile dye houses in Faisalabad and Karachi, and agricultural distributors throughout Pakistan.

We supply standard 85% industrial / commercial grade formic acid, which is the benchmark for tanneries and textile dyeing in Pakistan.

How is formic acid packed and shipped safely?

Formic acid is packaged in heavy-duty acid-resistant polyethylene carboys (35 kg), blue drums (250 kg), and 1000 L IBC totes with vented caps for safe transport.
