Where people use it

Paints & Primers

Widely used in red oxide metal primers, anti-corrosion coatings, architectural paints and wood finishes for strong opacity.

Concrete & Tuff Tiles

Alkali-resistant, non-fading pigment for interlocking pavers, cement tiles, terrazzo flooring and coloured concrete blocks.

Ceramics & Glazes

Plastics & Rubber

Heat-stable inorganic pigment for PVC pipes, rubber compounds, footwear soles and polymer masterbatches.

About this chemical

Iron Red Oxide (Fe₂O₃) is one of the most durable inorganic pigments available. Because it resists sunlight, weather, alkalis and high processing temperatures, paint manufacturers, tuff tile factories and ceramic workshops across Pakistan rely on it for consistent shade and coverage. Questions

What grades of Iron Red Oxide do you supply?

We supply high-purity synthetic Red Iron Oxide (such as 130 and 190 series) as well as natural micronized grades depending on whether you need it for paints, concrete pavers, tiles or ceramics.

Is your Iron Red Oxide suitable for interlocking pavers and cement tiles?

Yes. Our Red Iron Oxide is alkali-resistant, UV-stable and weatherproof, so it does not fade in sunlight when mixed into concrete pavers, tuff tiles, terrazzo and coloured cement.
