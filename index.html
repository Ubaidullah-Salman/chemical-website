Ask a question
Where can I buy silver nitrate in Pakistan?

From our Lahore plant. Purity is as per your request, and the material is available as crystals and as powder. Customised bulk orders for either form are quoted the same day — send the percentage, the form and the kilograms.

What if the chemical is not on this website?

Send the name anyway. The pages here are the regulars, not the whole range. If we can supply it, we will say so plainly.

Do you sell small packs and bulk?

Both. A bench might want 10 g. A factory might want a drum. Put the quantity in the message and we will quote that pack.

Do you deliver across Pakistan?

Yes. Fast nationwide cargo leaves the Lahore warehouse for Karachi, Faisalabad, Gujranwala, Peshawar, Sialkot and other industrial towns. A COA and SDS go with the batch.

Is copper sulphate the same as Neela Thota?

Yes. Copper sulphate is widely known in Pakistan as Neela Thota (Neela Thotha, نیلا تھوتھا). We supply the powder and the pentahydrate crystals, in 1 kg lab packs and 25 kg bags.
