A Lahore chemical house, fifty years in the trade.

Sulman Traders manufactures and supplies industrial and laboratory-grade chemicals. The company was established in 1974 and is now run by the third generation of the same family.

From a small counter to a factory floor

The business began as a modest trading desk. Buyers in the jewellery, plating and farm trade wanted material they could trust, in the pack they actually used, without waiting on an importer. That desk grew into manufacturing: silver nitrate, copper salts and the oxides the ceramic and glass kilns in Punjab were already calling for.

Three generations later the work sits in a modern factory and warehouse in Lahore. Batches are made, tested and held there, then moved by cargo into Karachi, Faisalabad, Gujranwala, Peshawar, Sialkot and the smaller industrial towns in between. If a buyer is in the city, the warehouse can also load a local pickup.

The range is industrial and laboratory-grade chemical manufacturing. Production follows an ISO 9001 quality alignment. Every batch is tested so the purity, the particle size and the written specification match what was ordered — not a neighbouring lot that happened to be on the pallet.

The same standard covers the names Pakistan’s workshops already use. Copper sulphate is supplied as the powder buyers ask for as Neela Thota (Neela Thotha, نیلا تھوتھا) and as pentahydrate crystals. Silver nitrate is made as crystals and as powder, with purity as per the request. Black copper oxide leaves at 99% for ceramics, glass and batteries. Black cobalt oxide (Co₃O₄) is the high-purity black powder the glaze books specify.
